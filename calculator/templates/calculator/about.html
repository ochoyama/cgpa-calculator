🎓 About A-DEVS

Helping students understand their academic progress.

A-DEVS CGPA Calculator provides students with simple academic tools for calculating, tracking and planning their university performance.

Why A-DEVS was created

University students often need to calculate their GPA and CGPA manually or rely on tools that only provide a single calculation without helping them understand their overall academic progress.

A-DEVS CGPA Calculator was created to make this process simpler. Students can calculate semester results, maintain academic records and use planning tools to better understand how their academic decisions may affect their progress.

Our goal is to make academic information easier for students to understand and manage.

More than a CGPA calculator

A-DEVS combines CGPA calculation with academic record keeping and planning tools.

Students can keep track of semester results, experiment with possible future grades, set CGPA goals and organize important academic information from one account.

What we stand for

A-DEVS is designed around simplicity, accessibility and useful academic planning.

🎯

To provide students with simple and accessible tools that help them calculate, understand and plan their academic performance.
