How can we help?

Click any question below to view the answer.

A-DEVS CGPA Calculator is an academic management platform that helps students calculate GPA and CGPA, save semester results and use academic planning tools.

The core CGPA calculation and academic management features are currently available for students to use without charge.

GPA is calculated using your course units and grade points. Each course grade is converted to a grade point, multiplied by the course unit and divided by the total course units for the semester.

The calculator currently uses a five-point grading scale where A = 5, B = 4, C = 3, D = 2, E = 1 and F = 0.

Yes. Registered students can save semester results to their account and view them later from their academic record and dashboard.

The application includes offline support for saving result information temporarily on your device. When your connection returns, supported saved information can be synchronized with your account.

The CGPA Goal Planner helps you estimate the academic performance you may need in future semesters to work toward a target CGPA.

The Grade Simulator lets you experiment with possible future grades and see how different scenarios may affect your academic performance.

When you are logged in and save your results, your academic information is stored in the application's database so it can be associated with your account.
