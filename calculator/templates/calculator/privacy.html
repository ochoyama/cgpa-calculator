Privacy Policy

This page explains how A-DEVS CGPA Calculator handles information when students use the platform.

Last updated: September 5, 2026

1. Introduction

A-DEVS CGPA Calculator provides academic tools that help students calculate GPA and CGPA, maintain academic records and plan their academic performance.

This Privacy Policy explains the types of information that may be collected when you use the platform and how that information is used.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the platform, information may include:

  • Account information such as your username, email address or other registration details.
  • Student information used to manage your academic profile.
  • Course information including course codes, credit units, grades and semester results.
  • Academic planning information such as CGPA goals, reminders and related study data.
  • Profile images you choose to upload.
  • Messages submitted through the Contact page.

3. How We Use Information

  • Provide GPA and CGPA calculation services.
  • Save and display your academic records.
  • Provide planning and reminder tools.
  • Maintain, secure and improve the platform.
  • Respond to support messages and feedback.

4. Local Device Storage

Some features may temporarily store information on your device using browser storage. This can include unfinished calculator drafts or information waiting to synchronize when a connection becomes available.

5. Cookies and Sessions

The platform may use cookies that are necessary for authentication, session management and security protections such as CSRF protection.

If analytics or advertising services are introduced later, this policy may be updated to explain any additional cookies or technologies they use.

6. Third-Party Service Providers

The platform may rely on hosting, database and infrastructure providers to operate the service. Those providers may process information as necessary to provide their services.

7. Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to help protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their passwords and login credentials.

8. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain records, respond to support requests, protect the platform and comply with applicable obligations.

9. Sale of Personal Information

A-DEVS CGPA Calculator does not intentionally sell users' personal information.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the platform, its features or service providers change. The latest revision date will be shown near the top of this page.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how the platform handles information, please use the Contact page.
